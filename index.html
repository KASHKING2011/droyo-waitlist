The short version

A few things you may be wondering.

Droyo is built to make product content feel repeatable—not technical.

📦What do I give Droyo?

A product photo, your avatar choice, and a short product brief. The template handles the repeatable creative direction from there.

✨Do I need to write prompts or edit every video?

No prompt engineering is required. Choose a ready-made format and control the product, avatar, and direction without rebuilding the workflow.

▶Where can my videos go?

Droyo is designed around TikTok, TikTok Shop, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts, with connected social workflows for consistent posting.

🛍️Does it work for TikTok Shop products?

Yes. Product-led formats and batch creation are designed for sellers who need more angles to test without more shoots.
